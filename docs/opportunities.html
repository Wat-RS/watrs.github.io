Opportunities

The Watershed Remote Sensing Lab is based in the Department of Geography, Earth and Environmental Sciences at UNBC, in Prince George, British Columbia. We work at the intersection of watershed hydrology, cryospheric science, remote sensing and environmental sensor design.

Graduate students

Prospective graduate students (Master’s only at this time) interested in these themes are welcome to get in touch. Graduate work in the lab is normally through the Natural Resources and Environmental Studies (NRES) program at UNBC.

When you reach out, please include a short description of your research interests and why they fit the lab and your CV, highlighting any relevant fieldwork, programming or geospatial experience.

Graduate projects could include the following themes (these are themes, not research questions):

  • Remote sensing in watershed science

    • Advancing the use of lidar in hydrology

    • Testing new satellite sensors for hydrological applications

    • Thermal remote sensing of stream and lake temperature, from drones and from space, including remote-sensing-driven estimates of water temperature

  • Open, low-cost monitoring

    • Democratizing hydrometric monitoring with open-source software

    • Developing low-cost, DIY, automated water samplers to understand water quality

    • Extending the RemoteLogger platforms

  • Hydrology of northern BC

    • Progress towards a nested physical hydrological model for northern BC

    • The thermal regime of permafrost in northern BC and its impact on hydrology

    • Assessing the impact of glacier change on future water resources in northern BC

    • Extending the watershedBC platforms

Students are also welcome to propose their own project within these themes — if your interests overlap with the lab’s work, get in touch.

Undergraduate students

Undergraduate research assistant positions come up through the academic year and over the summer, including NSERC Undergraduate Student Research Awards (USRA). Current and past assistants are listed on the People page. Get in touch if you would like to be considered.

Professional mentorship

I mentor articling agrologists through the BC Institute of Agrology Professional Agrologist Mentorship Program. Current and past mentees are listed on the People page.
